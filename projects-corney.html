Designing Interior/Product of Mobile Clinic Module for COVID-19 Patients

12.2020 – 02.2021 @UNIST · Master Graduation Project
Supervisors Chajoong Kim, Youngwoo Park
Role & Responsibility Group project (3 people); Desk research, user research, Rendering, UI design

This project aims to design a product for patient in mobile clinic settings. To alleviate patient anxiety and support emotional regulation, psychological ad environmental stressors were identified and sensory interaction strategies such as light and sound were proposed for adaptive, human-centered medical spaces. As a result, an ASMR-inspired ambient lighting system was designed.

Background

How might we reduce negative emotions of COVID-19 patient in negative pressure ward?

The goal of this project was to design a product that improves the patient experience in negative pressure wards, which are used during the COVID-19 pandemic. Despite the development of mobile clinics, issues with the patient experience in these wards still persist. The focus of this project was to reduce negative emotions associated with being in a negative pressure ward and thus improve the overall patient experience during this difficult time.
